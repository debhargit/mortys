My interviews

Interviews you are on the panel for. After each one, fill in your scorecard: the hiring team sees everyone’s scores side by side.

My jobs

Jobs you are the hiring manager or an interviewer for. As hiring manager you can read each applicant, their CV and the panel’s scores, add your notes and compare them; hiring staff move them on.

Requisitions

The case for a new job, approved before it is posted. Once approved, “Post it” makes the job posting.

Applicants

Applicants get an automatic reply with a link where they can add documents or withdraw. Those not hired are deleted (details, CV and documents) after the retention period. Click one to open it.
