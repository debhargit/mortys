About us

Seven years of genuine parts.

Morty's Auto Parts Co Ltd has been operating for 7 years from 112C Waltham Park Road, Kingston. We specialise in the sale of new auto parts and supplies for Toyota, Suzuki, Mitsubishi, Subaru, Nissan, Honda & Mazda.

We offer computerized parts locating, free battery check, and free tyre change with purchase. Our general auto servicing includes front end, brake repair, shocks, struts, pressing in & out bushing, joints, and injector cleaning.
