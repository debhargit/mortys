Common Questions

Quick answers from the team

Don't see your question? Call us or send a message via WhatsApp — we'll get back fast.

How long does it take to source a part you don't have in stock?

Most common parts ship from our trusted suppliers within 3–7 business days, often faster for popular vehicles. For rarer parts, we'll quote a realistic timeline upfront — no surprises.

Do you install the parts you sell?

Yes — our in-house service center can fit most parts you buy from us. We share a clear labor estimate when we quote the part. Wheel alignment and balancing are usually done same-day.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, debit and credit card, and bank transfer at our Hagley Park Road counter. Mobile money options are available on request. WhatsApp orders are confirmed by call before payment.

Do your used parts come with any kind of warranty?

Tested used parts (engines, transmissions, alternators and similar) come with a 30-day fitment guarantee. New OEM and aftermarket parts carry the manufacturer's warranty — we'll show you the paperwork at pickup.

Do you deliver parts outside of Kingston?

Yes — we can arrange courier delivery to most parishes for a fee, or you can collect from Hagley Park Road. We'll confirm timing and cost when we quote your part.

Can I get a same-day service appointment?

For oil changes, wheel alignment and balancing, and basic diagnostic scans — usually yes. For larger jobs (brakes, A/C, engine work) we book ahead so the right tech and parts are ready when you arrive.
